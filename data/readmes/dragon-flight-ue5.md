# Dragon Flight (UE5)

An Unreal Engine 5 remake of [dragon-flying-sim](https://github.com/faarisaahmed/toothless-sim-remaster):
fly a life-size dragon over a 45 km northern archipelago — Lumen, Nanite,
virtual shadow maps, volumetric clouds, a real day/night cycle and weather.

> **Status: work in progress.** See `Docs/STATUS.md` for what works, what's
> untested, and what's next.

- **Setup for playing on a Windows gaming PC:** `Docs/SETUP_Windows.md`
- **How the world is made:** `Docs/World.md` (baked from the web game's own terrain code)
- **Flight model:** `Docs/FlightModel_FromWeb.md` (every constant, ported line for line)

Built against **UE 5.8** (developed on an M4 Pro Mac mini).
